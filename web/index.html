A biomechanical rhythm machine and synth for the M-VAVE FM-1

It should sound like its own disturbing machinery.

▶ Play it here Hear the demos Install on your FM-1 Source

In development. It runs here exactly as on the FM-1 (the same code, compiled to WebAssembly), and it builds for the FM-1, passes its tests and runs in the FM-1 emulator, but it has not yet been tried on a real FM-1.

Play it here

This is the firmware itself, the same C as on the FM-1, running on a web FM-1. Press Start, then PLAY: RITUAL 00, THE AWAKENING. Turn SELECT for the focus (the kit, or one organ), PRESETS for the scene, and ALGO (ALGORITHM) for the nervous system's LOOP. REC while it plays records the keys.

HEART F3–B3 · BONE C4–F#4 · TEETH G4–C#5 · CLAW D5–G5

Press Start: the firmware loads when you do.

The computer's keyboard, once you have clicked the FM-1: white keys Z…/ and Q…Y, black ones the row above. Space PLAY, Enter REC, Esc HOME, Shift held is SEL (SHIFT).
The knobs: drag up or down, scroll, or the arrow keys. Hold SEQ, LFO, ARP or HOME (press and keep the mouse down) and the white keys become that page's buttons: the steps, the nerve's WRITE / RESET / FUSE, BURST, the mutes.
What it keeps (its settings, the scenes you SAVE) stays in this browser. A MIDI keyboard plays it: channel 1 the focus, 2–10 HEART … NOISE, recorded as the keys are.
All the controls
SELECTThe focus: DRUM (the kit, four zones of keys), or one organ, HEART … NOISE. The keys play it; REC writes to it.
SELSHIFT while held (a tap keeps the knobs' second row).
ALGORITHMThe Turing register's LOOP, on every page: 100 % locked, 0 % double-locked (the pattern, then its inverse), between: it slips.
PRESETSThe scene: RITUAL 00 THE AWAKENING, 01 FROZEN, 02 STREAM, 03 WALL (playing: on the next bar). SHIFT: a factory ritual into this scene (DATA, STATIC CULT, SPLICE, VOID, …).
K1–K4The page's four (SHIFT: the second four).
HOMEMIX: the six channels; again: the focused channel's sends. Held: white keys 1–6 mute the channels.
SEQThe grid. Held: the white keys are the focused track's 16 steps; tap to set or clear, hold one and turn a knob for a p-lock.
EDIT / ENVSYN: BODY, TEXTURE, MOTION, DECAY (a noise organ, SHIFT: SCATTER, TONE, DRIFT); again: the organ's own depth. ENV: its envelope; on a drum, SHIFT: its SOUND (one of sixteen noise sounds, or TISSUE).
LFOMUT: the spine. Held: white keys WRITE 0, WRITE 1, RESET, FUSE, LOCK.
FX / GLODelay, reverb, crush (again: deeper). GLO: tempo, swing, quantise; SHIFT: OVERDUB or REPLACE, the step length.
ARPHeld: BURST on the white keys: STUTTER, INVERT, FREEZE, SPASM.
PLAY / RECStart, stop (SHIFT: PANIC). REC: playing, LIVE REC; stopped, STEP REC (KNOB 1 the cursor); SHIFT + REC: OVERDUB / REPLACE.
OCT− / OCT+The keys' octave. SHIFT: UNDO, REDO.
SAVEThe scene (here: into this browser; on the FM-1: into flash, two slots, when it is quiet).

Hear the demos

Each one is the firmware itself on a computer, its keys and knobs played by a script, its sound as it goes out over USB: nothing added, nothing sampled. The picture is the spectrum, low at the bottom: click it to jump.

How it works

TISSUE: the drums

No kick, snare or hat. Each hit is an excitation (a PRESSURE impulse, seeded FRACTURE clicks, coloured FRICTION, a VENT of air) through damped modal resonators, then an asymmetric flesh curve. HEART is a hydraulic piston, BONE a fracture whose resonance bends, TEETH a ratchet, CLAW a gesture through ribs.

The nervous system

A 16-bit shift register clocked by the transport, after the Turing Machine (Music Thing Modular). On the big knob: LOCK, slip, random, double lock. It moves the texture and the scrape, adds ghost hits (RATTLE), picks notes (WAIL), and never moves what you played; FUSE writes its ghosts in.

The keys into the memory

LIVE REC while it plays, STEP REC while it stands, p-locks from the knobs, overdub, replace, erase, sixteen undos. A live note keeps the moment you played it; the track's quantise places it when it plays.

Nine organs, six channels

DRUM (with a shared rib resonator), BASS, DRONE, SPASM, VOICE (three formants moving U O A E I), NOISE. BLOOD DELAY, BILE REVERB, MARROW CRUSH as sends; PUMP ducks it all under the kick. Four scenes; integers only, on one core without an FPU.

Rhythm made of noise

NOISE, and any drum turned to it. The sequencer locks each step's noise, so the same noise comes back and is the groove; LOOP lets it slip. SCATTER gives each step its own slice, TONE puts the band on the note, a ratchet stutters the same noise. Under it: five sources, a filter that morphs and pings, a sweep, bursts, drive, a crusher, width; sixteen sounds to start from.

Install

  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer (they can talk to the FM-1 over USB MIDI).
  2. Connect the FM-1 directly to the computer with a USB data cable, and switch it on.
  3. Close other apps that use MIDI, tick the box, press Install and allow MIDI when the browser asks.
