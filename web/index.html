EARLY ALPHA

A biomechanical rhythm machine and synth for the M-VAVE FM-1

It should sound like its own disturbing machinery.

▶ Play it here Hear it Install on your FM-1 Source

EARLY ALPHAIt runs here exactly as on the FM-1 (the same code, compiled to WebAssembly), it builds for the FM-1 and passes its tests, but it has not yet been tried on a real FM-1. Its sounds, its controls and the way it keeps its scenes will still change: a new version can forget what an older one saved.

Play it here

The firmware itself, the same C as on the FM-1, on a web FM-1. Press Start, then PLAY. PRESETS turns between its four scenes; SEL held and PRESETS turned loads any of twenty-two factory rituals. SELECT picks the focus (the kit, or one organ), ALGO the nervous system's LOOP; REC while it plays records the keys.

HEART F3–B3 · BONE C4–F#4 · TEETH G4–C#5 · CLAW D5–G5

Press Start: the firmware loads when you do.

The computer's keyboard, once you have clicked the FM-1: white keys Z…/ and Q…Y, black ones the row above. Space PLAY, Enter REC, Esc HOME, Shift held is SEL (SHIFT).
The knobs: drag up or down, scroll, or the arrow keys. Hold SEQ, LFO, ARP or HOME (press and keep the mouse down) and the white keys become that page's buttons: the steps, the nerve's WRITE / RESET / FUSE, BURST, the mutes.
What it keeps (its settings, the scenes you SAVE) stays in this browser. A MIDI keyboard plays it: channel 1 the focus, 2–10 HEART … NOISE, recorded as the keys are.
All the controls
SELECTThe focus: DRUM (the kit, four zones of keys), or one organ, HEART … NOISE. The keys play it; REC writes to it.
SELSHIFT while held (a tap keeps the knobs' second row).
ALGORITHMThe Turing register's LOOP, on every page: 100 % locked, 0 % double-locked (the pattern, then its inverse), between: it slips.
PRESETSThe scene: RITUAL 00 THE AWAKENING, 01 RUST, 02 ABYSS, 03 ASH (playing: on the next bar). SHIFT: one of twenty-two factory rituals into this scene (FROZEN, GRINDER, SPINE, TENDON, LAMENT, OSSUARY, SHRINE, MIRAGE, …).
K1–K4The page's four (SHIFT: the second four).
HOMEMIX: the six channels; again: the focused channel's sends. Held: white keys 1–6 mute the channels.
SEQThe grid. Held: the white keys are the focused track's 16 steps; tap to set or clear, hold one and turn a knob for a p-lock.
EDIT / ENVSYN: BODY, TEXTURE, MOTION, DECAY (SHIFT: a noise organ's SCATTER, TONE, DRIFT; a synth's MODEL: HUM, STEEL, PISTON, GLASS, BELL, CHOIR, BLOOD, BUBBLE, SLIME, DUB, MASS, CLUSTER); again: the organ's own depth. ENV: its envelope; on a drum, SHIFT: its SOUND (a noise sound, a kick, or TISSUE).
LFOMUT: the spine; again: WAIL, its range, RUPTURE (the register cutting into the music). Held: white keys WRITE 0, WRITE 1, RESET, FUSE, LOCK.
FX / GLODelay, reverb, crush (again: deeper; again: the reverb's SPACE, DUCK, WARP: a wavering tape). GLO: tempo, swing, quantise; SHIFT: OVERDUB or REPLACE, the step length. GLO again: KEY, SCALE (twelve), the focused synth's ARP.
ARPHeld: BURST on the white keys: STUTTER, INVERT, FREEZE, SPASM; keys 5 to 10 SCAR, the last 0.37 s played back: REPEAT 1/16, REPEAT 1/8, REVERSE, SMEAR, SCREW, DROP.
PLAY / RECStart, stop (SHIFT: PANIC). REC: playing, LIVE REC; stopped, STEP REC (KNOB 1 the cursor); SHIFT + REC: OVERDUB / REPLACE.
OCT− / OCT+The keys' octave. SHIFT: UNDO, REDO.
SAVEThe scene (here: into this browser; on the FM-1: into flash, two slots, when it is quiet).

A look

The keys into the memory

Nine tracks: four drums of pressure, fracture, friction and vents, four synths, a noise. LIVE REC while it plays, STEP REC while it stands, p-locks, overdub, replace, undo.

Organs and landscapes

Each synth its own voice or a landscape: HUM, STEEL, PISTON, GLASS, BELL, CHOIR, BLOOD, BUBBLE, SLIME, DUB, MASS, CLUSTER. Noise as percussion, a deep kick, a delay, a reverb of five rooms.

The nervous system

A shift register after the Turing Machine, clocked by the transport: it locks and slips the noise, picks notes, arps, and cuts into the music (RUPTURE), the same cuts each time round while it is locked.

Hear it

Each one is the firmware itself on a computer, its keys and knobs played by a script, its sound as it goes out over USB: nothing added, nothing sampled. The picture is the spectrum, low at the bottom: click it to jump.

Install

  1. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer (they can talk to the FM-1 over USB MIDI).
  2. Connect the FM-1 directly to the computer with a USB data cable, and switch it on.
  3. Close other apps that use MIDI, tick the box, press Install and allow MIDI when the browser asks.
